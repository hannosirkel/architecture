# Gas City closeout — 2026-10-15

The operator declared the Gas City proof of concept done on 2026-10-15. This
is closure by operator direction, not a claim that gates G1 to G4 passed. Gate
G0 passed on 2026-10-09; gates G1 to G4 were not run, and no decision record
was written. The operator's choice to deploy Gas City stands in for the G4
decision.

## What replaced the trial

The private [`devbox`](../../../universe/repositories.yaml) repository now
deploys Gas City and Orca on devbox. It configures everything inside the
devbox VM and is governed as an `inventory-private` repository.

| Component | Version | Deployed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gas City (`gc`) | 1.5.0 | `devbox` |
| Beads (`bd`) | 1.3.1 | `devbox` |
| Dolt | 2.1.7 | `devbox` |
| Orca server | v1.4.218 | `devbox` |

The tool versions are the ones the trial pinned in
[the G0 evidence](../../../initiatives/active/gas-city/evidence/2026-10-09-install-and-first-runs.md).

The trial city at `~/gascity/lab` is replaced by a deployed city at
`~/gascity/city`. It has one rig per governed repository under
`~/gascity/rigs/`.

The trial's machine-local state was archived on devbox under `~/backups`.

## Where the record lives

The contract, state, concept, usage guide, example, and dated evidence stay in
[`initiatives/active/gas-city/`](../../../initiatives/active/gas-city/state.yaml).
The `devbox` repository cites the usage guide and the G0 evidence for its tool
pins, so moving them would break those references.
`state.yaml` records the status.

Runtime behaviour, versions, and deployment of Gas City and Orca are owned by
the `devbox` repository from now on.
